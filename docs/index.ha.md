# Menene Soundscape?
Soundscape manhaja ce ta kyauta mai budaddiyar tushe wadda take amfani da sautin sarari na 3D domin taimaka wa mutane masu naƙasar gani su gina fahimtar muhallinsu kuma su yi tafiya cikin kwarin gwiwa.

Maimakon bayar da umarnin juyawa daya bayan daya, Soundscape yana aiki kamar hasumiyar haske ga kunnuwa. Ta belun kunne na stereo, masu amfani suna jin sautuka masu zuwa daga bangaren wurare na hakika, ta haka suke gina taswirar tunani mai wadata game da muhallinsu.

- **Sanarwa ta atomatik** – Yayin da kake tafiya, manhajar tana sanar da mahadar hanyoyi, shaguna, tashoshin bas, wuraren shakatawa da sauran wuraren muhimmanci da ke kusa.
- **Hasken sauti** – Saita inda za ka je sai ka ji sauti mai ci gaba na sarari yana jan ka wajen. Kidan yana canzawa idan ka fuskanci hanya daidai.
- **Sanin wuri** – Danna maballi domin jin abin da ke kewaye da kai a kowanne bangare, wace hanya kake kai, ko abin da ke gaba.
- **Alamomi da hanyoyi** – Ajiye wuraren da kake so ka kuma kirkiri hanyoyin tafiya a kafa tsakaninsu. Manhajar tana bi da kai a kan hanyar, tana kuma matsawa zuwa wuri na gaba ta atomatik idan ka isa.
- **Duban titi** – Bincika yankin da ba ka sani ba a kama-da-wane kafin ka ziyarce shi, domin samun kwarin gwiwa da sanin makamar wurin.

Manhajar tana ci gaba da aiki a baya idan wayar tana aljihu ko cikin jaka – babu bukatar rike ta ko duban allo. Kyauta ce gaba daya kuma babu talla, kuma tana iya aiki ba tare da intanet ba ta amfani da yankunan taswirar da aka sauke. Manhajar tana aika kididdigar amfani da rahotannin gazawa domin mu gano matsaloli mu gyara su - [manufar sirri]({{ "/privacy-policy.html" | relative_url }}) tana bayyana ainihin abin da ake tattarawa.

Da farko [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/product/soundscape/) ne suka kirkiri Soundscape a kan iOS, kuma an yi amfani da ita sosai kafin a mai da ita budaddiyar tushe a 2022. [Scottish Tech Army](https://www.scottishtecharmy.org) ta ci gaba da samar da ita a kan iOS, ta kuma sake rubuta ta a matsayin sabuwar manhaja mai budaddiyar tushe don Android da iOS, wadanda ke amfani da lamba guda.

## Beta na jama’a
Sababbin fasaloli suna fara zuwa a sakin beta, kuma kowa zai iya shiga.

### iOS
Manhajar iOS da ke kan App Store har yanzu ita ce tsohuwar manhajar da aka gina bisa ta Microsoft. Sabon sakin Soundscape na iOS, wanda aka gina daga lamba ɗaya da manhajar Android, ana samunsa ne kawai a matsayin beta ta jama’a ta manhajar TestFlight ta Apple. Yana maye gurbin manhajar iOS da ake da ita, kuma yana riƙe alamomi da hanyoyin da ka adana - duba [sauyawa daga tsohuwar manhajar iOS]({{ "/ios-upgrade-from-legacy.html" | relative_url }}) (da Turanci) don ganin abin da ya canza. Don shiga:

1. Ka saka [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) daga App Store a iPhone ɗinka.
2. A iPhone ɗin, ka buɗe hanyar haɗi <https://testflight.apple.com/join/XFb7C5JN>.
3. TestFlight zai buɗe ya ba ka damar saka beta ta Soundscape. Ka karɓi gayyatar, sannan ka saka manhajar.

### Android
1. Ka buɗe <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/org.scottishtecharmy.soundscape> yayin da kake shiga da asusun Google da ake amfani da shi a wayarka, ka yi rajista a matsayin mai gwaji.
2. Ka saka ko sabunta Soundscape daga [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.scottishtecharmy.soundscape) kamar yadda aka saba. Zai iya ɗaukar ’yan mintoci kafin a ba da beta, don haka idan har yanzu kana ganin sigar da aka saki, ka ɗan jira ka sake dubawa.

Daga nan sabuntawar beta za su riƙa zuwa ta Play Store kamar kowace sabuntawa. Don daina gwaji, ka koma hanyar haɗin nan ka fita daga shirin.

Don Allah ka aika duk wata matsala da ka samu a kowace beta zuwa adireshin tallafi da ke ƙasa.
