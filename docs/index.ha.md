# Menene Soundscape?
Soundscape manhaja ce ta kyauta mai budaddiyar tushe wadda take amfani da sautin sarari na 3D domin taimaka wa mutane masu naƙasar gani su gina fahimtar muhallinsu kuma su yi tafiya cikin kwarin gwiwa.

Maimakon bayar da umarnin juyawa daya bayan daya, Soundscape yana aiki kamar hasumiyar haske ga kunnuwa. Ta belun kunne na stereo, masu amfani suna jin sautuka masu zuwa daga bangaren wurare na hakika, ta haka suke gina taswirar tunani mai wadata game da muhallinsu.

- **Sanarwa ta atomatik** – Yayin da kake tafiya, manhajar tana sanar da mahadar hanyoyi, shaguna, tashoshin bas, wuraren shakatawa da sauran wuraren muhimmanci da ke kusa.
- **Hasken sauti** – Saita inda za ka je sai ka ji sauti mai ci gaba na sarari yana jan ka wajen. Kidan yana canzawa idan ka fuskanci hanya daidai.
- **Sanin wuri** – Danna maballi domin jin abin da ke kewaye da kai a kowanne bangare, wace hanya kake kai, ko abin da ke gaba.
- **Alamomi da hanyoyi** – Ajiye wuraren da kake so ka kuma kirkiri hanyoyin tafiya a kafa tsakaninsu. Manhajar tana bi da kai a kan hanyar, tana kuma matsawa zuwa wuri na gaba ta atomatik idan ka isa.
- **Duban titi** – Bincika yankin da ba ka sani ba a kama-da-wane kafin ka ziyarce shi, domin samun kwarin gwiwa da sanin makamar wurin.

Manhajar tana ci gaba da aiki a baya idan wayar tana aljihu ko cikin jaka – babu bukatar rike ta ko duban allo. Kyauta ce gaba daya, babu talla ko tattara bayanai, kuma sigar Android tana iya aiki ba tare da intanet ba ta amfani da yankunan taswirar da aka sauke.

Da farko [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/product/soundscape/) ne suka kirkiri Soundscape a kan iOS, kuma an yi amfani da ita sosai kafin a mai da ita budaddiyar tushe a 2022. [Scottish Tech Army](https://www.scottishtecharmy.org) ta ci gaba da samar da ita a kan iOS, ta kuma sake rubuta ta a matsayin manhajar Android mai budaddiyar tushe.

# Soundscape a harshenka
Muna son mu tallafa wa harsuna da yawa gwargwadon iko, don haka mun fara amfani da [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/soundscape-android/) mai kyau domin taimakawa wajen fassara manhajar. Mun fara da harsunan da ake tallafa wa a iOS, amma akwai sabbin rubutu a manhajar Android, don haka a yawancin harsuna akwai fassarorin da suka bata. Kowane irin taimako wajen kara su, ko ma kara sabbin harsuna, za a karba da godiya. Bayanin yadda wannan ke aiki yana [nan](https://docs.weblate.org/en/latest/user/translating.html). Idan ka bude asusun Weblate, kana zabar harsunan da za ka iya fassarawa, kuma idan manhajar Soundscape ba ta tallafa wa wannan harshen a yanzu, za ka samu zabin kara shi a babban [shafin](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/soundscape-android/android-app/) manhajar.
