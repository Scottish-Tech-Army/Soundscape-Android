# What is Soundscape?
Soundscape is a free, open-source app that uses 3D spatial audio to help people with visual impairments build awareness of their surroundings and navigate more confidently.

Rather than giving turn-by-turn directions, Soundscape acts like a lighthouse for the ears. Through stereo headphones, users hear sounds coming from the direction of real-world places, building a richer mental map of their environment.

- **Automatic callouts** - As you walk, the app announces nearby intersections, shops, bus stops, parks, and other points of interest.
- **Audio beacon** - Set a destination and hear a continuous spatial sound pulling you toward it. The tone changes when you're pointing the right way.
- **Location awareness** - Tap a button to hear what's around you in each direction, what road you're on, or what's coming up ahead.
- **Markers and routes** - Save favourite locations and create walking routes between them. The app guides you along the route, automatically moving to the next waypoint as you arrive.
- **Street preview** - Explore an unfamiliar area virtually before visiting, to build confidence and familiarity.

The app carries on working in the background when the phone is in a pocket or bag - no need to hold or look at a screen. It is completely free and has no ads, and it can work offline using downloaded map regions. The app does send usage statistics and crash reports so that we can find and fix problems - the [privacy policy]({{ "/privacy-policy.html" | relative_url }}) explains exactly what is collected.

Soundscape was originally developed on iOS by [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/product/soundscape/) and was widely used before being open sourced in 2022. The [Scottish Tech Army](https://www.scottishtecharmy.org) kept it available on iOS and has rewritten it as a new open-source app for both Android and iOS, with the two sharing one codebase.

## Public betas
New features arrive in the beta releases first, and anyone can join them.

### iOS
The iOS app on the App Store is still the older Microsoft-based app. The new iOS release of Soundscape, built from the same code as the Android app, is only available as a public beta through Apple's TestFlight app. It replaces the existing iOS app and keeps your saved markers and routes - see [upgrading from the legacy iOS app]({{ "/ios-upgrade-from-legacy.html" | relative_url }}) for what has changed. To join:

1. Install [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) from the App Store on your iPhone.
2. On the same iPhone, open the link <https://testflight.apple.com/join/XFb7C5JN>.
3. TestFlight opens and offers to install the Soundscape beta. Tap Accept, then Install.

### Android
1. Open <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/org.scottishtecharmy.soundscape>, signed in to the Google account that's used on your phone, and tap Become a tester.
2. Install or update Soundscape from the [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.scottishtecharmy.soundscape) as normal. It can take a few minutes for the beta to be offered, so if you still see the released version, leave it a while and check again.

Beta updates then arrive through the Play Store like any other update. To stop testing, go back to the same link and leave the programme.

Please send any problems you find with either beta to the support address below.

## Soundscape support
The email address <soundscape@scottishtecharmy.support> links straight into our help desk system. Please send in any feedback you have, whether it's a feature request or a bug report. You'll receive an automated email in response and we'll get pinged that there's a new ticket to look at and we'll try and answer it as quickly as possible.

## Soundscape in your language
Soundscape is available in 46 languages, but most of the translations have not yet been checked by a native speaker. If you speak one of them, we'd love your help: the quickest way is to answer the short [questionnaire for your language]({{ "/help-translate/" | relative_url }}), which takes about ten minutes. You can also suggest improvements directly on [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/soundscape-android/androidkmp/), where the translations are managed ([how Weblate works](https://docs.weblate.org/en/latest/user/translating.html)).
