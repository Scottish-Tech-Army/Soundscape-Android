# Tips

To find nearby bus stops, select *Places Nearby* and choose *Public Transport*.

If you take the same bus often, save your stops as markers. Set a beacon on the stop where you get off, and Soundscape tells you how far away it is as you ride.

You can mute the beacon and still hear how far away the place is. Soundscape says the distance from time to time as you move.

To explore a place before you go, open its *Location Details* and select *Simulate Location*. Soundscape then behaves as if you were there.

For turn-by-turn directions, open the *Location Details* of a place and select *Open in Maps App*. Soundscape keeps running while the other app gives directions.
