# Nearby Markers

*Nearby Markers* calls out up to four of your markers, the ones closest to you. You hear each one from its own direction, with its distance. Use it to get your bearings from places you already know. Markers more than about 2 kilometres away are left out.

If you are standing still, first point the top of your phone the way you are facing. If your phone is locked, or Soundscape is not on the screen, hold your phone flat as well.
