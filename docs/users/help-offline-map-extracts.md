# Offline map extracts

Soundscape can download regions of map data to your phone so that it keeps working when you have no internet connection. The map below shows the regions that are currently available to download. They come in three flavours:

- **Country** — the whole country in a single extract.
- **Region** — a state, province or other top level subdivision of a country.
- **City** — a small extract covering a city and the towns immediately around it.

To check a place, type its name in the search box below. Every extract whose name, or list of towns, matches is listed with the same name, size and town list shown in the **Offline maps** screen inside the app, along with the larger region and country extracts that also cover it. The search works with a screen reader or keyboard; the map after it shows the same extracts for anyone who prefers to look.

On the map, tap anywhere to see every extract that covers that point: a country, the region within it, and any city extract there are all listed together.
