# Fassarar Soundscape zuwa Hausa — wasu tambayoyi

> Ku aika amsoshinku ta imel zuwa **soundscapeAndroid@scottishtecharmy.support**, kuma ku rubuta harshen a cikin taken imel ɗin.


*Hausa translation — questions for native-speaker reviewers. This sheet is
bilingual on purpose: the Hausa below was machine-written, so the English
lines are there in case it's unclear.*

Sannu, kuma mun gode da amincewa ku duba wannan.
*Hello, and thank you for agreeing to look at this.*

Soundscape manhaja ce ga makafi da masu raunin gani: yayin da kuke tafiya, tana
faɗa da murya abin da ke kewaye da ku. An yi fassarar Hausa ba tare da taimakon
mai jin harshen ba, don haka ra'ayinku yana da matuƙar muhimmanci.
*Soundscape is an app for blind and partially sighted people. It says aloud
what is around you as you walk. The Hausa was made without a native speaker.*

**Yadda za ku amsa:** ku amsa ta imel, ku rubuta lambar tambaya ("Q2: …").
Ba sai kun amsa duka ba.
*Reply by email quoting the question number. You don't need to answer
everything.*

Kusan komai **na'ura ce ke karanta shi da murya**. Abin da ya fi muhimmanci
shi ne ya zama a fili kuma na halitta *idan an ji shi*.
*Almost everything is read aloud by a speech synthesiser, so how it sounds
matters most.*

---

**Q1 — Gaba ɗaya** *(Overall)*
Shin Hausar ana fahimtarta, ko tana kama da fassarar na'ura?
*Is the Hausa understandable, or does it read like machine translation?*

**Q2 — "Matsayi"** *(Waypoint)*
Ana kiran wuraren tsayawa a kan hanya "matsayi". Shin wannan yana nufin wurin
tsayawa a hanya?
*Stops along a route are called «matsayi». Does that mean a stop on a route?*

**Q3 — "Sanarwa" da "Siginar Sauti"** *(Callout, Beacon)*
Gajerun saƙonnin murya game da kewaye ana kiransu "sanarwa". Sautin da ke
fitowa daga inda za ku je ana kiransa "Siginar Sauti". Suna da kyau?
*Callouts are «sanarwa» and the beacon is «Siginar Sauti». Are they natural?*

**Q4 — "Titin X, ta juya hagu"** *(Description or instruction?)*
A mahaɗar hanya, manhajar tana cewa "Titin X, ta juya hagu". Shin wannan yana
kama da umarni ("ku juya hagu"), ko bayanin inda titin ya nufa?
*Does «ta juya hagu» sound like an instruction to turn, or a description of
where the road goes?*

**Q5 — Girmamawa** *(Register)*
Manhajar tana magana da "ku" ("Kun shirya!"). Shin wannan daidai ne?
*The app uses the respectful plural. Is that right?*

**Q6 — Wani abu kuma?** *(Anything else)*

Mun gode sosai!
