# Tafsiri ya Kiswahili ya Soundscape — maswali machache

> Tuma majibu yako kwa barua pepe kwenda **soundscapeAndroid@scottishtecharmy.support**, na utaje lugha kwenye kichwa cha barua.


*Swahili translation — questions for native-speaker reviewers. This sheet is
bilingual on purpose: the Swahili below was machine-written, so the English
lines are there in case it's unclear.*

Habari, na asante kwa kukubali kuangalia hili.
*Hello, and thank you for agreeing to look at this.*

Tafsiri ya Kiswahili ilifanywa bila msaada wa mzungumzaji asilia, kwa hiyo maoni
yako ni ya thamani sana. **Huhitaji kuijua programu wala kuisakinisha:** kila
swali linaeleza maandishi yanasikika lini, yanasema nini kwa Kiingereza, na
yanasikikaje sasa kwa Kiswahili.
*The Swahili was made without a native speaker. You don't need to know or install
the app: each question says when you hear the text, the English, and how it
sounds in Swahili now.*

## Soundscape ni nini?
*What is Soundscape?*

Soundscape ni programu ya simu ya bure kwa watu wasioona na wenye uoni hafifu.
Inatumika wakati wa kutembea, na vipokea sauti masikioni, simu mara nyingi ikiwa
mfukoni. Haitoi maelekezo hatua kwa hatua kama ramani ya kuongoza ("pinda
kushoto"); inakueleza kwa sauti kilicho karibu, ili mtu ajielekeze mwenyewe.
*A free phone app for blind and partially sighted people, used while walking with
headphones. It doesn't give turn-by-turn directions; it says aloud what's nearby.*

Maneno machache yanayojitokeza kwenye maswali:
*Some terms used in the questions:*

- **Tangazo la sauti** *(callout)*: ujumbe mfupi wa sauti kuhusu kitu unachopita,
  kwa mfano "Mkahawa", "Njia ya miguu karibu na Barabara ya Moi" au "Unatembea
  kuelekea kaskazini kwenye Barabara ya Moi". Unasikika kutoka upande kilipo.
  *A short spoken message about something you pass, heard from its direction.*
- **Beacon ya Sauti** *(audio beacon)*: ukichagua unakoenda, sauti inayojirudia
  inasikika masikioni kutoka upande huo. Ukigeuka, sauti nayo "inasogea", kwa hiyo
  unaweza kufika kwa kusikiliza.
  *A repeating sound from the direction of your destination; it moves as you turn.*
- **Alama** na **njia** *(marker, route)*: mahali palipohifadhiwa, na mfululizo wa
  mahali hapo ambao beacon inakuongoza moja baada ya jingine.
  *Saved places, and a sequence of them the beacon leads you through.*

Watu wasioona hutumia simu kwa **kisoma skrini** (TalkBack kwenye Android,
VoiceOver kwenye iPhone): sauti ya kompyuta husoma kila kitufe na kila maandishi.
Kwa hiyo maandishi ya programu karibu kila mara *husikilizwa*, hayasomwi, mara
nyingi katikati ya kelele za barabarani. Muhimu zaidi ni yawe mafupi, wazi na ya
kawaida yanaposikika.
*Blind users hear everything through a screen reader, often in street noise.*

### Q1 — "Beacon" *(Beacon: English word)*

**Linasikika lini:** kwenye vitufe, mipangilio na ziara ya utangulizi: "Sasa
unaweza kusikia beacon ya sauti. Inachezwa kutoka mwelekeo wa mahali
unapoelekea."
*On buttons, in settings and in the tutorial.*

**Kwa Kiingereza:** "Audio Beacon".

**Linavyosikika sasa:** "Beacon ya Sauti" (neno la Kiingereza).

**Swali:** Je, ni sawa, au kuna neno la Kiswahili bora zaidi?
*Keep the English «beacon», or use a Swahili word?*

### Q2 — Kwa jumla *(Overall)*

**Swali:** Je, Kiswahili cha programu kinaeleweka, au kinasikika kama tafsiri ya
mashine? Mifano iliyo juu yote inatoka kwenye programu.
*Does the app's Swahili read like machine translation? The examples above are real
app text.*

### Q3 — "Tangazo la sauti" *(Callout)*

**Linasikika lini:** ni jina la jumbe fupi za sauti zilizoelezwa juu, hasa kwenye
mipangilio: "Matangazo ya Sauti Kiotomatiki".
*The name of the short spoken messages, mostly in settings.*

**Kwa Kiingereza:** "Callout", "Automatic Callouts".

**Wasiwasi wetu:** "tangazo" linaweza kumaanisha pia matangazo ya biashara.
*«tangazo» can also mean an advert.*

**Swali:** Ni la kawaida? Kama sivyo, ungesema nini?
*Is it natural? If not, what would you say?*

### Q4 — "Vivutio" *(Landmarks)*

**Linasikika lini:** kwenye mipangilio ("Sehemu na Vivutio") na kwenye matangazo.
Maana yake ni bustani, makanisa, misikiti na mahali pengine panaposaidia
kujielekeza.
*In settings («Sehemu na Vivutio») and callouts: parks, churches, mosques and other
places that help orientation.*

**Kwa Kiingereza:** "Landmarks", "Places and Landmarks".

**Wasiwasi wetu:** "vivutio" ni vivutio vya utalii.
*«vivutio» means tourist attractions.*

**Swali:** Je, neno hili linafaa, au ni la kitalii mno?
*Does it fit, or is it too touristy?*

### Q5 — Kitu kingine? *(Anything else)*

Kama sentensi yoyote inasikika kama tafsiri kutoka Kiingereza, ni ndefu mno au
haieleweki, tuambie.
*Anything that sounds translated, too long or unclear, please tell us.*

Asante sana!
