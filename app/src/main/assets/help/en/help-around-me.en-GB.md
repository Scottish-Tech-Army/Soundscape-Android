# Around Me

*Around Me* calls out up to four places near you: one ahead, one to the right, one behind and one to the left. You hear each one from its own direction, with its distance. Use it to get your bearings when you arrive somewhere.

If you are standing still, first point the top of your phone the way you are facing. If your phone is locked, or Soundscape is not on the screen, hold your phone flat as well.
