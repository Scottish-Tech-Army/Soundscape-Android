# My Location

*My Location* tells you where you are. It says which way you are facing or travelling, then the street or address you are at. Use it when you step off a bus, leave a building or lose your bearings.

If you are standing still, first point the top of your phone the way you are facing. If your phone is locked, or Soundscape is not on the screen, hold your phone flat as well.
