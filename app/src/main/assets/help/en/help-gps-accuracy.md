# GPS accuracy

## GPS accuracy

Soundscape can only describe your surroundings as well as your phone knows where you are. Accuracy of 30 feet or better is good. Up to 60 feet is ok. Beyond that, callouts may name the wrong side of the street or arrive late.

Accuracy is usually worst indoors, among tall buildings, and in the first minute after you go outside. If the figure below stays poor while you are outside in the open, turn your phone's location services off and on again.
