# Umarnin Murya na Siri

A iPhone, kuna iya neman Siri ya yi abubuwa a cikin Soundscape ba tare da taɓa wayarku ba. Misali, jin sanarwa, fara tafarki, ko saita sigina a kan alama.

Soundscape yana amsawa da muryarsa, da sanarwowinsa da sautukan siginarsa na yau da kullum. Don haka har yanzu kuna jin kowane sauti daga ɓangaren wurin.

Ku yi amfani da umarnin murya idan wayarku tana cikin aljihu ko hannayenku a cike suke. Suna aiki yayin da Soundscape yake bayan fage da kuma yayin da wayarku take a kulle. Neman sanarwa ba ya buɗe manhajar.

Siri ne yake sauraro, ba Soundscape ba. Don haka Soundscape ba ya taɓa amfani da makirufon ɗinku, belun kunnenku na Bluetooth kuma suna riƙe da ingantaccen sautinsu.

## Yadda Ake Nema

Kowane umarni yana da siffa ɗaya: *“Soundscape”*, sai rukuni, sai zaɓinku. Misali, *“Soundscape surroundings around me”*.

Kuna iya kuma tsayawa bayan rukunin, sai Siri ya tambaye ku wane zaɓi kuke so. Don haka kowane rukuni yana aiki kamar menu na magana. Rukunoni bakwai ne kawai za a tuna.

## Abin da Za Ku Iya Nema

*“Soundscape surroundings”* — sai *Wurina*, *Kewaye da Ni*, *Gaba da Ni*, *Alamomi na Kusa*, *Sanar da Siginar Sauti* ko *Bayanin Siginar Sauti*.

*“Soundscape route”* — sai *Matsayi na Gaba*, *Matsayi na Baya*, *Kashe Siginar Sauti* ko *Tsayar*.

*“Soundscape start route”* — sai sunan ɗaya daga cikin tafarkunanku.

*“Soundscape beacon”* — sai sunan ɗaya daga cikin alamominku.

*“Soundscape stop beacon”*.

*“Soundscape detail”* — sai *Shiru*, *Muhimmi*, *Sauƙaƙe* ko *Cikakke*. *Shiru* yana kashe sanarwowi kai tsaye.

*“Soundscape list”* — sai *Tafarkuna*, *Alamomi* ko *Umarnai*. *Umarnai* yana karanta muku wannan jerin.

## Yana da Kyau a Sani

Umarnan suna bayyana kuma a cikin manhajar Shortcuts da kuma cikin manhajar Settings ta iOS a ƙarƙashin *Siri*. A can kuna iya ganin dukan jerin kuma ku ƙara kalmominku ga kowane umarni.
